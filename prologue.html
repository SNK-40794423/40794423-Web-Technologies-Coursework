Prologue

They haven't been gotten a single personal request so they are left with having to accept the quests posted at guild hub. And since this is a festival season at neighbour city so there are no special quests being posted with all the dangers being taken care of weeks ago. Since all the quest are just basic quests, they aren't getting enough coins to cover all the expenses and needing to accept multiple quests daily.


Unbeknownst to them, a sealed letter got send to the guild with instructions to hand them over to El Quatre Venator without opening the content while they're being very desperate at their inn thinking for their next move.
